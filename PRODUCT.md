# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two readers land on this page together. Builders (platform and AI engineers) want to ship agents fast on any model, with their real tools. Security and platform leads in regulated orgs (BFSI, government, PSU) want every agent sandboxed, every action on record, and the option to run it on their own infrastructure. Both arrive from a link, scan for under a minute, and decide whether to join the waitlist or book a demo.

## Product Purpose

AgentZ is a Zero Trust Agentic AI Security Platform by AccuKnox. It is a platform, never a "framework". Teams build, run, automate and govern production agents in a sandbox that trusts nothing by default. It runs as SaaS, on-prem or air-gapped, works with any model on the customer's own key, keeps credentials out of the agent, and records every action. Success on this site is a visitor who understands the value in one scroll and clicks "Join the waitlist" or "See the platform".

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, calm. The site should feel like an Apple product page for a security platform: one idea per view, few words, real proof. The headline that frames the value: "Ship agents fast. Stay in control."

## Anti-References

- Fake browser or OS window chrome around screenshots.
- Floating glass notification chips, decorative glassmorphism, gradient text, side-stripe borders, big-number hero metrics.
- Tiny uppercase eyebrows above every section, mono kickers, tag chips and three tiers of label text on one card.
- Identical card grids of icon, heading and paragraph.
- Green anywhere. Auto-popup modals.

## Design Principles

1. One idea per tile. A headline and one line, then a visual that proves it.
2. Show the mechanism. A sandbox, a key swap or a flow beats an adjective.
3. Practice what we preach. Default deny, least privilege and audit show up as real states, not slogans.
4. Color carries meaning. Each value group owns one hue from the bot's palette (blue, violet, cyan, red).
5. Don't hide the enterprise answers. On-prem, air-gapped and bring-your-own-model are front and center.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text at 4.5:1 or better. Every animation has a reduced-motion path that shows the end state. Carousels work with keyboard and touch, and never auto-advance faster than a person can read.
